Narrated zone lore for **[Spoken](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken)**'s Places module, in Korean. This addon is data only — it does nothing on its own.

It plays while Spoken Places is showing its lore in **Korean**, and never under another language's text. It installs beside the English pack, **[Spoken Audio: Places](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-audio-places)**, rather than replacing it, so a player who switches languages keeps the narration for both.

## Installing

Install **Spoken** first; this pack is inert without it. Extract into `Interface/AddOns` and leave the folder name as shipped (`SpokenZonesAudio_koKR`) — Spoken finds packs by folder name.

Spoken Places reads your client's language when it has lore in it, and `/spp lang koKR` picks Korean on any client.

## Compatibility

For **WoW Forever**, Classic Era and Anniversary, with Spoken 3.0.0 or later.
