⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced in Traditional Chinese.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names Spoken Player and every Traditional Chinese sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

[Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player), which carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones and Spoken Books inside it, and the Traditional Chinese voice packs:

| Module | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken Quests | quest dialogue | [Chinese (Traditional) Spoken Quests](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/chinese-traditional-spoken-quests) |
| Spoken Quests | NPC gossip | [Chinese (Traditional) Spoken Quests: Gossip](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/chinese-traditional-spoken-quests-gossip) |
| Spoken Zones | zone and subzone lore | [Spoken Zones Audio: Chinese (Traditional)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-zones-audio-zhtw) |
| Spoken Books | books, letters, notes and plaques | [Spoken Books Audio: Chinese (Traditional)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books-audio-zhtw) |

## Language

Spoken speaks in your client's language by default. To hear Traditional Chinese on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **Fallback Language** decides what plays for a line the Traditional Chinese packs do not hold.

English has its own set: [Spoken Everything](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken).
