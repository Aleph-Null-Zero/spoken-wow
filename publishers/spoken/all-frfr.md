⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced in French.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names Spoken Player and every French sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

[Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player), which carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones and Spoken Books inside it, and the French voice packs:

| Module | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken Quests | quest dialogue | [French Spoken Quests: Alliance](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/french-spoken-quests-alliance)<br>[French Spoken Quests: Horde](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/french-spoken-quests-horde)<br>[French Spoken Quests: Shared](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/french-spoken-quests-shared) |
| Spoken Quests | NPC gossip | [French Spoken Quests: Gossip](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/french-spoken-quests-gossip) |
| Spoken Zones | zone and subzone lore | [Spoken Zones Audio: French](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-zones-audio-frfr) |
| Spoken Books | books, letters, notes and plaques | [Spoken Books Audio: French](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books-audio-frfr) |

The quest dialogue comes as three packs, one per faction plus the quests both sides share, and this installs all of them: it does not know which side you play. Remove the other faction's pack afterwards if you want the disk space back.

## Language

Spoken speaks in your client's language by default. To hear French on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **Fallback Language** decides what plays for a line the French packs do not hold.

English has its own set: [Spoken Everything](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken).
