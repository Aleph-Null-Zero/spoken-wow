⚠️ NOT for manual download, install only via CurseForge app ⚠️

**Install this and everything is voiced in Latin American Spanish.** It holds no audio and no code — it is a few kilobytes that names Spoken Player and every Latin American Spanish sound pack as a dependency, so your addon manager fetches the lot.

## What it installs

[Spoken Player](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-player), which carries Spoken Quests, Spoken Zones and Spoken Books inside it, and the Latin American Spanish voice packs:

| Module | Voices | Its audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spoken Quests | quest dialogue | [Spanish (AL) Spoken Quests: Alliance](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spanish-al-spoken-quests-alliance)<br>[Spanish (AL) Spoken Quests: Horde](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spanish-al-spoken-quests-horde)<br>[Spanish (AL) Spoken Quests: Shared](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spanish-al-spoken-quests-shared) |
| Spoken Quests | NPC gossip | [Spanish (AL) Spoken Quests: Gossip](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spanish-al-spoken-quests-gossip) |
| Spoken Zones | zone and subzone lore | [Spoken Zones Audio: Spanish (AL)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-zones-audio-esmx) |
| Spoken Books | books, letters, notes and plaques | [Spoken Books Audio: Spanish (AL)](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken-books-audio-esmx) |

The quest dialogue comes as three packs, one per faction plus the quests both sides share, and this installs all of them: it does not know which side you play. Remove the other faction's pack afterwards if you want the disk space back.

## Language

Spoken speaks in your client's language by default. To hear Latin American Spanish on another client, pick it under **Voice Language** on Spoken's settings page; **Fallback Language** decides what plays for a line the Latin American Spanish packs do not hold.

English has its own set: [Spoken Everything](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/spoken).
